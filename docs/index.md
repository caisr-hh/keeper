# KEEPER

The KEEPER Synergy project [Knowledge Creation for Efficient and Predictable Industrial Operations](projects.md#keeper) is an overarching initiative strategically designed to scientifically profile research activities at Halmstad University and to ensure long-term collaboration between Halmstad University and key industrial partners in industrial artificial intelligence, thereby enabling efficient and predictable operations. The work combines machine learning research with real industrial data and close collaboration with companies and research partners. It mainly develops AI and machine
learning methods for turning largely unlabelled operational data into actionable industrial knowledge.

KEEPER entails research in the industrial track of the Center for Applied Intelligent Systems Research (CAISR). The KEEPER project is funded by KKS, the Knowledge Foundation, and balances business-relevant research questions defined by industry and state-of-the-art research in Machine Learning.

## Research

The core KEEPER Synergy question is "how to best do AI/ML-based knowledge creation from largely unlabelled industrial data that leads to more efficient industry operations?". This addresses topics related to intelligent vehicles, predictive maintenance, industrial time-series analysis, anomaly detection, federated and continual learning, and trustworthy AI on related industrial applications.

### The DataScape subproject
focuses on developing methods to improve data quality and maximise the value of available data for AI/ML applications. It explores how to identify and address data quality issues, such as missing values, noise, inconsistencies, and biases, and how to leverage unlabelled data through self-supervised and unsupervised learning to develop meaningful representations for downstream tasks. The subproject also investigates synthetic data generation to address data scarcity and class imbalance, improve the representation of rare events and operating conditions, and enable data sharing when access to original data is restricted by privacy, confidentiality, or ownership concerns. The overall aim is to make AI/ML models more reliable, data-efficient, and effective in real-world applications.

### The KnowScape subproject 
focuses on combining data-driven AI/ML methods with expert knowledge, domain understanding, and physical principles to develop more efficient, robust, and interpretable learning systems. It investigates how prior knowledge and domain constraints can be integrated into model architectures and learning processes, including approaches such as unsupervised meta-learning and physics-inspired machine learning. In addition, the subproject explores human-in-the-loop methods that incorporate expert input and collaborative feedback to guide learning, improve predictions, and adapt models to changing requirements. The overall aim is to develop AI systems that can learn more effectively from limited data by combining the strengths of machine learning, established domain knowledge, and human expertise.

### The DiversiScape subproject 
focuses on developing AI/ML methods that can handle heterogeneous data, diverse operating conditions, and differences between systems and application domains. It investigates how models can learn from data collected under varying conditions and how knowledge acquired in one domain can be transferred to new domains or systems, for example through transfer learning and domain adaptation. The subproject also explores learning from distributed data sources using approaches such as edge computing and federated learning, where data may need to remain locally stored due to communication, computational, privacy, or ownership constraints. The overall aim is to develop adaptable, scalable, and robust AI solutions that can generalise across different systems and operating environments while making effective use of distributed data and previously acquired knowledge.

## Selected connected projects

### FREEWAY

[FREEWAY](projects.md#freeway) develops asynchronous federated learning, edge processing and MLOps for heterogeneous
commercial-vehicle fleets.

### AIM-FLIX

[AIM-FLIX](projects.md#aim-flix) investigates AI-supported multi-echelon inventory management for automotive aftermarket
supply chains.

## Industrial collaboration

KEEPER research is carried out with industrial companies across transport, material handling, maritime equipment,
industrial communication and energy systems, together with research and innovation organisations. See the
[Partners page](partners.md) for the current overview.
