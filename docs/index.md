# KEEPER

KEEPER brings together research at Halmstad University on industrial artificial intelligence for efficient and
predictable operations. The work combines machine learning research with real industrial data and close collaboration
with companies and research partners.

## Research

KEEPER research addresses intelligent vehicles, predictive maintenance, industrial time-series analysis, anomaly
detection, federated and continual learning, trustworthy AI and related industrial applications.

## KEEPER Synergy project

The KKS-funded project
[Knowledge Creation for Efficient and Predictable Industrial Operations](https://www.hh.se/english/research/research-programmes/firp---future-industry-research-programme/research-projects-firp/keeper---knowledge-creation-for-efficient-and-predictable-industrial-operations-.html)
develops AI and machine learning methods for turning largely unlabelled operational data into actionable industrial
knowledge.
