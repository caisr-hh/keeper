# KEEPER

KEEPER brings together research at Halmstad University on industrial artificial intelligence for efficient and
predictable operations. The work combines machine learning research with real industrial data and close collaboration
with companies and research partners.

KEEPER and the Center for Applied Intelligent Systems Research (CAISR) are separate but strongly overlapping research
frameworks. They involve many of the same people, research topics and industrial relationships, and KEEPER builds on
experience and networks developed around CAISR. The KKS-funded KEEPER Synergy project is one important project within
the broader work presented on this site.

## Research

KEEPER research addresses intelligent vehicles, predictive maintenance, industrial time-series analysis, anomaly
detection, federated and continual learning, trustworthy AI and related industrial applications.

### Industrial AI and predictive maintenance

We develop methods that help organisations understand equipment condition, anticipate faults and improve how complex
assets are operated and maintained. Application areas include heavy-duty vehicles, buses, forklifts, maritime equipment,
industrial networks and heat pumps.

### Learning from industrial data

Industrial data is often noisy, heterogeneous and only weakly labelled. Our research studies how AI and machine learning
can identify useful patterns and create knowledge from the data available during everyday operations rather than relying
on ideal laboratory datasets.

### Trustworthy and deployable machine learning

We investigate federated, continual, explainable and edge-based learning. The goal is to develop methods that can work
across fleets, devices and organisations while remaining useful to operators, technicians, managers and other domain
experts.

## Selected projects

### KEEPER Synergy project

[Knowledge Creation for Efficient and Predictable Industrial Operations](projects.md#keeper) develops AI and machine
learning methods for turning largely unlabelled operational data into actionable industrial knowledge.

### FREEWAY

[FREEWAY](projects.md#freeway) develops asynchronous federated learning, edge processing and MLOps for heterogeneous
commercial-vehicle fleets.

### AIM-FLIX

[AIM-FLIX](projects.md#aim-flix) investigates AI-supported multi-echelon inventory management for automotive aftermarket
supply chains.

## Industrial collaboration

KEEPER research is carried out with industrial companies across transport, material handling, maritime equipment,
industrial communication and energy systems, together with research and innovation organisations. See the
[Partners page](partners.md) for the current overview.
