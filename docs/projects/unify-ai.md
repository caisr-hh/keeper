# UNIFY-AI

## Time-Series Foundation Models and Robust MLOps for Industrial Fleet Data

### Project Overview

UNIFY-AI is a collaboration between Halmstad University and Volvo Group to develop reusable AI methods for industrial
time-series data. The project combines **time-series foundation models** with **robust machine learning operations
(MLOps)** to support aftermarket and vehicle uptime services. It connects advances in machine learning with the
practical requirements of developing, deploying and maintaining models in industrial environments.

### Motivation

Vehicle fleets generate large amounts of operational data that can support predictive services. However, these data vary
across vehicles, customers and operating conditions. Differences in data quality, availability and usage patterns make
it challenging to develop models that perform reliably across the fleet.

Developing a separate model for each application also requires substantial effort in data preparation, training and
maintenance. Time-series foundation models offer an opportunity to learn shared patterns from diverse data and adapt
this knowledge to specific tasks. Realising this potential requires both effective modelling methods and reliable
processes for managing the complete machine learning lifecycle.

### Objectives and Approach

UNIFY-AI addresses these challenges through two closely connected objectives:

- **Develop a time-series foundation model** that learns from heterogeneous fleet data and can be adapted to relevant
  industrial prediction tasks.
- **Establish robust MLOps processes** that support reproducible development, systematic evaluation, deployment and
  continued model monitoring.

The project brings together industrial data preparation, model research and practical validation. Data analysis will
examine differences across fleet data sources and identify factors affecting model performance and transferability.
Model development will investigate how shared representations can be learned from heterogeneous time series and adapted
to specific applications.

In parallel, MLOps development will establish workflows connecting data preparation, training, evaluation and model
maintenance. The modelling methods and operational workflows will be brought together in an industrial demonstration to
assess their practical usefulness.

### Collaboration

Halmstad University contributes expertise in machine learning, time-series modelling and MLOps. Volvo Group contributes
industrial knowledge, fleet-data expertise and application requirements. This collaboration ensures that research
questions are informed by operational needs and that proposed methods are evaluated in a relevant industrial setting.

### Expected Outcomes and Impact

Expected outcomes include a time-series foundation model, reproducible MLOps workflows and an industrial demonstration.
Together, these outputs aim to reduce repeated development effort, improve knowledge reuse across fleet applications and
support more reliable AI services.

By addressing both modelling and operational requirements, UNIFY-AI aims to enable scalable and maintainable AI for
aftermarket and vehicle uptime services.
