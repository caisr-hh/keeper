# LEAD-AI: Empowering Adult Learners and Educators through Artificial Intelligence

**LEAD-AI** is a European project co-funded by the Erasmus+ Programme, bringing together partners from France, Sweden,
Germany, and Bulgaria to strengthen Artificial Intelligence (AI) competences in adult education and professional
environments.

As Generative AI transforms the way we work, learn, and communicate, LEAD-AI aims to make these technologies more
accessible, understandable, and useful. The project focuses on developing practical, flexible, and inclusive learning
opportunities that help adult learners and educators use AI confidently, critically, and responsibly.

## Project objectives

LEAD-AI seeks to bridge the gap between the growing availability of AI technologies and the skills needed to use them
effectively. Its main objectives are to:

- **Strengthen AI literacy:** Provide adult learners with foundational knowledge of Generative AI, its capabilities,
  limitations, and risks.
- **Develop practical AI competences:** Support the application of AI tools in everyday professional activities,
  including productivity, communication, and decision-making.
- **Promote responsible AI adoption:** Encourage critical thinking, ethical awareness, and informed use of AI
  technologies.
- **Empower adult educators:** Develop a Train-the-Trainer programme that equips educators with the pedagogical
  competences and resources needed to teach AI effectively.
- **Support accessible and personalised learning:** Create flexible training pathways adapted to different competence
  levels, professional backgrounds, and learning needs.

## Expected results and impact

LEAD-AI aims to contribute to the digital transformation of adult education through several complementary outcomes:

- **A pedagogical framework** providing methodologies and assessment strategies for effective AI capacity building.
- **Practical training modules** covering AI fundamentals, prompting, evaluation, ethics, professional applications, and
  multimodal AI.
- **A Train-the-Trainer toolkit** supporting educators in delivering high-quality, responsible AI education.
- **An AI-enhanced e-learning platform** offering accessible learning resources, interactive activities, and
  personalised learning pathways.
- **Recognition of acquired competences** through practical assessments and optional digital micro-credentials.

Through these results, the project expects to improve AI literacy, encourage lifelong learning, strengthen professional
adaptability, and support the responsible integration of AI across different sectors.

## Project progress

Since its launch in September 2025, the LEAD-AI consortium has been working collaboratively to establish the foundations
of its educational programme.

Key activities undertaken or currently being developed include:

- **Needs analysis:** Gathering insights into AI competences, learning preferences, and professional challenges across
  target groups.
- **Participatory co-design:** Engaging educators, professionals, and other stakeholders to ensure the training reflects
  real-world needs.
- **Pedagogical and assessment design:** Developing learning pathways, competence levels, assessment approaches, and a
  Train-the-Trainer evaluation methodology.
- **Training content development:** Preparing educational modules and practical learning activities addressing different
  levels of AI competence.
- **Digital platform development:** Working towards an online environment to support flexible and interactive AI
  learning.

As the project progresses, these activities will inform the refinement, implementation, and evaluation of the training
programme and its supporting resources.

## European collaboration

LEAD-AI is implemented by a consortium of four European partners: **HAIKARA** (France, project coordinator), **Halmstad
University** (Sweden), **ESCP Business School** (Germany), and **EVROPROJECT** (Bulgaria).

By combining expertise in education, research, artificial intelligence, and digital innovation, the consortium aims to
contribute to a more inclusive, digitally competent, and AI-ready European society.

## Project information

- **Duration:** September 2025 – February 2028
- **Programme:** Erasmus+ Cooperation Partnerships in Adult Education
