# Projects

KEEPER overlaps with a wider body of industrial AI research at Halmstad University. The projects below are related
through people, methods or application areas, but they do not all belong to the single KKS-funded KEEPER Synergy
project.

## Current projects

### KEEPER

**Full title:** Knowledge Creation for Efficient and Predictable Industrial Operations\
**Period:** October 2024–September 2028\
**Project manager:** Sepideh Pashami\
**Funder:** The Knowledge Foundation\
**Industrial partners:** Alfa Laval, HMS Networks, Toyota Material Handling Europe, Volvo Buses, Volvo Group Connected
Solutions, Volvo Group Trucks Technology and Volvo Trucks

KEEPER develops AI and machine learning methods for creating actionable knowledge from raw, largely unlabelled
operational data. Its application areas include trucks, buses, forklifts, pumps and separators, and industrial and
network assets. The research is organised around DataScape, KnowScape and DiversiScape.

[Official KEEPER project information](https://www.hh.se/english/research/research-programmes/firp---future-industry-research-programme/research-projects-firp/keeper---knowledge-creation-for-efficient-and-predictable-industrial-operations-.html)

### FREEWAY

**Full title:** Automating asynchronous federated learning and edge computing for efficient vehicle operation analytics\
**Period:** August 2025–August 2027\
**Project lead:** Yuantao Fan\
**Funder:** Vinnova, FFI programme\
**Partners:** Volvo Group Trucks Technology, RISE and Stream Analyze

FREEWAY develops asynchronous federated learning, edge processing and MLOps for heterogeneous vehicle fleets. Use cases
include energy forecasting, vehicle-operation profiling, activity recognition and anomaly detection.

[Official FREEWAY project information](https://www.hh.se/english/research/research-programmes/firp---future-industry-research-programme/research-projects-firp/freeway---automating-asynchronous-federated-learning-and-edge-computing-for-efficient-vehicle-operation-analytics.html)

### AIM-FLIX

**Full title:** AI-driven inventory management for flexible and efficient next-generation supply chains\
**Period:** November 2025–December 2028\
**Project manager:** Abdallah Alabdallah\
**Funder:** Vinnova\
**Partners:** Volvo Service Market Logistics and Rejmes Transportfordon

AIM-FLIX builds on AIM-TRUE to investigate reinforcement-learning-supported multi-echelon inventory management,
forecasting, shared stocking and survival-analysis-based decision support for automotive aftermarket supply chains.

[Official AIM-FLIX project information](https://www.hh.se/english/research/research-programmes/firp---future-industry-research-programme/research-projects-firp/aim-flix---ai-driven-inventory-management-for-flexible-and-efficient-nextgen-supply-chains.html)

### AI-MARiE

**Full title:** AI-Based Machine Activity Recognition on the Edge\
**Funder:** Åforsk\
**Industrial context:** Toyota Material Handling

AI-MARiE studies machine activity recognition for forklifts and material-handling equipment using operational sensor
data, representation learning, domain adaptation and edge analytics. The end date is omitted because it has not been
confirmed in a current authoritative public record.

[Åforsk funded-project listing](https://aforsk.com/en/PreviousWinners)

### LEAD-AI

**Full title:** Empowering adult learners and educators through artificial intelligence\
**Period:** September 2025–February 2028\
**Local project manager:** Sławomir Nowaczyk\
**Funder:** Erasmus+

LEAD-AI strengthens AI literacy, responsible generative-AI use, adult learning and train-the-trainer capacity through a
European collaboration.

[Official LEAD-AI project information](https://www.hh.se/english/research/research-programmes/firp---future-industry-research-programme/research-projects-firp/lead-ai-empowering-adult-learners-and-educators-through-artificial-intelligence.html)

### Nordic MIL Simulator

**Full title:** A Customizable Solution and Simulation Platform for Maritime Alternative Energy Transition\
**Period:** October 2025–December 2027\
**Project coordinator:** CetaSol AB\
**Funder:** Swedish Energy Agency

The project develops a maritime simulation and decision-support platform for evaluating alternative energy systems with
real operational data and validated models. Mikael Andersson contributes from Halmstad University; project coordination
sits with CetaSol AB.

[Swedish Energy Agency project record](https://www.energimyndigheten.se/forskning-och-innovation/data-om-finansiering-av-forskning-och-innovation/projektdatabas/sokresultat/?registrationnumber=2025-204381)

### WELDIFY

**Full title:** Deep-tech driven inclusive and socially-aware circular material joining and welding for a safe future\
**Funder:** Erasmus+

WELDIFY connects circular-material welding, intelligent manufacturing, training, human–machine interaction and workforce
development. Halmstad University participates in the wider European consortium.

[Official WELDIFY project site](https://weldify.eu/)

### ENGADE

**Funder:** Swedish Research Council

ENGADE investigates foundational methods and diffusion-based approaches for anomaly detection in time-series data. Exact

## Recent and completed projects

### PREMA-HEAPS

**Full title:** Predictive Maintenance and Fault Detection for Efficient Heat Pumps\
**Period:** September 2024–August 2026\
**Local project leaders:** Sepideh Pashami and Sławomir Nowaczyk\
**Funder:** Swedish Energy Agency\
**Partners:** RISE and ClimaCheck

PREMA-HEAPS developed predictive maintenance, fault detection, root-cause analysis and methods for understanding
measurement uncertainty in commercial heat pumps.

[Official PREMA-HEAPS project information](https://www.hh.se/english/research/research-programmes/firp---future-industry-research-programme/research-projects-firp/prema-heaps---predictive-maintenance-and-fault-detection-for-efficient-heat-pumps.html)

### PRIDE

**Full title:** Ranking Data for AI-Driven Precision Diagnostics in Heavy-Duty Trucks\
**Period:** August 2024–July 2026\
**Project manager:** Sławomir Nowaczyk\
**Funder:** Vinnova, FFI programme\
**Partner:** Volvo Technology AB

PRIDE explored AI, natural-language processing and data-driven methods for improving heavy-duty vehicle diagnostics
using under-utilised service and ranking information.

[Official PRIDE project information](https://www.vinnova.se/p/ranking-data-for-ai-driven-precision-diagnostics-in-heavy-duty-trucks-pride/)

### FREEPORT

**Full title:** Federated Learning and Edge Processing for Safe and Efficient Operation\
**Period:** September 2023–August 2025\
**Halmstad University project manager:** Sławomir Nowaczyk\
**Funder:** Vinnova, FFI programme\
**Partners:** Volvo Group Trucks Technology, Volvo Trucks, Boliden, Stream Analyze, RISE and AI Sweden

FREEPORT advanced federated learning and edge processing for heavy-duty vehicle operation. Its results provide
predecessor context for FREEWAY.

[Official FREEPORT project information](https://www.vinnova.se/en/p/freeport-federated-learning-and-edge-processing-for-safe-and-efficient-operation/)
